# Cross-book render and reference-integrity audit

Read-only audit of `probability-statistics-bg.pdf`, the 15 `lectures/lecture_*.pdf` files, the full-book TeX driver/preamble/body sources, and the available `check_refs` result.

Required checks: page count and package readability; representative and risk-page visual inspection for page breaks, theorem boxes, equations, TikZ, tables, appendices, headers/footers, TOC, glyph loss, clipping/overlap, and numbering/reference survival. No source modification, PDF rebuild, re-export, or artifact marker is permitted. Scratch raster images are working material only and are not cited as audit evidence.
