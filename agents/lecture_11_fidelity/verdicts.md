## P0 proof review

For `0<σ²<∞` **and an MGF finite in a neighborhood of zero**, the displayed proof mechanism is sound: standardization gives IID `Y_j` with mean 0 and variance 1; the local expansion `M_{Y_1}(s)=1+s²/2+o(s²)` gives `M_{Z_n}(t)=(1+t²/(2n)+o(1/n))^n→e^{t²/2}` for fixed `t`; MGF convergence then yields the CLT in that narrower class. It does **not** prove the stated finite-variance theorem. Requiring existence for all real `t` in body 209 is stronger than necessary but sufficient; a neighborhood of zero suffices.
